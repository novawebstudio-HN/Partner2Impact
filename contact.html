Before the call

How to make 15 minutes count

What should I have ready?
Roughly how many donor records you hold, which CRM they live in, and last year’s fundraising total. That is enough for us to tell you something useful on the call.
Do you need access to our database?
Not for the health check — we can work from your description. If you decide to go ahead with a full audit, access is read-only and covered by a written agreement before anything is connected.
Is it really free?
Yes. It is a working conversation, not a sales call. If we are not the right fit we will say so.
What happens afterwards?
If it makes sense to continue, you get a scoped proposal covering the work, the timeline and the cost — no surprises.
