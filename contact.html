How to make 15 minutes count

What should I have ready?
Roughly how many donor records you hold, which CRM they live in, and last year’s fundraising total. That is enough for us to tell you something useful on the call.

Not for the health check up — we can work from your description. If you decide to go ahead with a full audit, access is read-only and covered by a written agreement before anything is connected.
Is it really free?
Yes. It is a working conversation, not a sales call. If we are not the right fit we will say so.
