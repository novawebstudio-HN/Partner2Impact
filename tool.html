01 — Hidden major donors

Stop finding out six months late

Static databases go stale the day you export them. The tool re-reads your records against life events, giving capacity and behavioral shifts, so supporters whose circumstances have changed reach your development team instead of sitting unnoticed.

  • Life events and public wealth signals
  • Giving capacity movement, up and down
  • Engagement and behavioral trend shifts

Predictive scoring engine

Every record gets a score you can sort by. Know exactly who to call today, who is at risk of leaving, and who is ready to upgrade.

Lapse-risk alerts

Silent churn surfaces as a flag, not a year-end surprise. Re-engage while the relationship is still warm.

Ask-amount guidance

Wealth indicators translate into a suggested range, so the ask matches the capacity instead of guessing low.

Portfolio views

Each gift officer sees their own ranked list — who to work this week, in priority order.

02 — New prospect discovery

Find the donors you haven’t met yet

Prospect research eats hours and often produces cold names. The tool works the other direction: it starts from what your best current donors have in common and finds warm, high-affinity leads that match your mission.

Your team spends its time building relationships with people who are already likely to say yes — instead of qualifying strangers.

Ask about prospect discovery

Mission affinity modelling

Leads scored on how closely they resemble the donors who already give to you.

Warm-path identification

Surfaces where an existing relationship in your network can open the door.

Capacity screening

Filters out names who look promising but cannot give at the level you need.

Exportable lists

Results flow back into your CRM so your existing workflow doesn’t change.

03 — How it fits

It works with the CRM you already pay for

No migration project, no retraining your team on new software. We connect to your existing system, clean and standardize what is there, and hand the intelligence back in a form your staff already knows how to use.

Connect

Clean

Duplicates merged, formats standardized, gaps flagged — before any modelling runs.

Score

Predictive models append scores, capacity ranges and risk flags to every record.

Refresh

Updates keep arriving, so the picture stays current instead of ageing in a report.
