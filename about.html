Common questions

Before you reach out

Is using donor data for predictive targeting ethically sound and compliant with privacy laws?
Data ethics and security are central to this framework. We work exclusively with aggregated, anonymized, and privacy-compliant datasets, adhering strictly to GDPR, CCPA, and donor privacy standards. Furthermore, when we upload prospect cohorts to media platforms like Meta or LinkedIn, the data is heavily encrypted and hashed. We never sell donor information.
Do we have to change our CRM?
No. The tool layers on top of the system you already use — Raiser’s Edge, Salesforce, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect and others. There is no migration project.
Our database is a mess. Is it too early for this?
That is the normal starting point, and cleaning it is step one of the work. The data health check up will tell you honestly whether the records are good enough to model yet, and what it would take to get there.
Can we get the consulting without the tool, or the other way around?
Yes, though they are designed to reinforce each other. Some organizations start with a one-off diagnostic and add the ongoing data work later.
How does an engagement start?
With a free 15-minute data health check up. You speak with Tracey and David, see a live demonstration, and get an honest read on whether we are the right fit.
